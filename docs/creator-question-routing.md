# Creator question routing — first bounded version

Creators can write their own concern, not only select a starter. The review step preserves their words and proposes an editable investigation focus when one supported set of English wording clues is present. Mixed concerns, explicit exclusions and unclear wording ask one priority question instead. Editing the investigation question clears the previous focus, including manually chosen focus, so it must be confirmed again.

This is deterministic wording routing, not general semantic understanding, an AI diagnosis or a guarantee of the creator's intended meaning. Incidental nouns without investigation wording are not routed. Wording outside the rules, other languages, implicit concerns and more complex exclusions may require manual selection or may yield an incorrect editable suggestion. No confidence percentage, inferred cause or fabricated answer is produced. Requested periods are preserved as input, not automatically applied to video selection.

The visible scope note states what the current workflow can do before collection. Public view comparisons remain descriptive; other focuses identify necessary evidence without claiming an implemented recommendation or private analytics connection. The confirmed focus continues through the existing backend inquiry contract. No additional external provider or secret is introduced.
