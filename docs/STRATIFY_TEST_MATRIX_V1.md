\## Scoring Guide



\### Benchmark Quality



\- Good: benchmarks clearly compete for similar viewer intent.

\- Mixed: some relevant, some noisy.

\- Poor: benchmarks are unrelated or too broad.



\### Useful Insight?



\- Yes: insight feels specific and actionable.

\- Mixed: partially useful but generic.

\- No: obvious, wrong, or not actionable.



\### Weak / Generic Output



Write exactly what felt weak.



Examples:

\- “Test high motion” felt too generic.

\- Benchmark videos were too broad.

\- Recommendation did not explain viewer psychology.

\- Evidence graph repeated the same pattern for top and lower benchmarks.



\---



\## What To Look For



1\. Does Stratify choose relevant benchmark videos?

2\. Does it explain why stronger videos work?

3\. Does the recommendation feel specific to the intro?

4\. Does the evidence actually support the test?

5\. Does the reasoning section reveal anything useful?

6\. Are top and lower benchmarks meaningfully different?

7\. Does the final test feel creator-actionable?
