# First real-channel pilot

Status: prepared, not yet run. Automated fixtures validate the implementation; they do not establish usefulness for a real creator. No YouTube API key is configured in the current execution environment. A channel link and genuine concern are still needed.

## Inputs

Use Rahul’s channel or a volunteer’s channel, as selected for this pilot. Obtain the channel link, the creator’s concern in their own words, and their goal or decision. Configure `YOUTUBE_API_KEY` in the local API process environment; do not paste it into chat or commit it. Use the existing private development setup and service-token/origin gates documented for the web migration. No deployment is required.

Start with public facts. Private analytics are optional and must come from the owner. For the matched-window step, use the exact selected video IDs, a completed first-24-hour/7-day/28-day window, and one consistent viewing metric. If impressions are added, use registered thumbnail impressions for the same windows. Keep missing data unavailable rather than entering zero. Manual values are unverified and are not persisted by the current form.

## Walkthrough

1. Let the creator enter and clarify the concern without coaching them toward an intro, packaging or views explanation. Check that the confirmed question still expresses their intent.
2. Compare channel identity, sampled video IDs, dates and available metrics against the source. Record sampling omissions or date coverage that prevents answering the concern. Do not interpret hidden subscriber counts as zero.
3. For a views concern, have the creator choose the videos and review topic/format comparability. Independently recompute the displayed medians and differences from the supplied figures. Review individual exceptions, not just the group summary.
4. Open every suggested step’s evidence record. Check that the observation supports that investigation, that limitations are visible, and that nothing claims a cause or prescribes an unsupported change.
5. Ask the creator to explain back what the answer means, what it cannot establish, and what they would do next. Record unclear wording, excessive scrolling, incorrect assumptions and whether the step helps their actual decision.

## Results to record

Record a short factual pilot note in a private working location: acquisition success/failure, source discrepancies, calculation discrepancies, misunderstood wording, missing evidence, usefulness of the next step, and changes needed. Clearly distinguish owner-supplied facts from verified public metadata. Do not publish private analytics, identifiers or screenshots in the public repository without the creator’s explicit instruction.

A successful calculation is not a successful pilot by itself. Finish when the creator can accurately describe the scoped finding and uncertainty and decide on a useful next investigation. Unsupported intent interpretation, causal claims, invented metrics or misleading certainty are findings to fix. This single-channel pilot cannot validate every channel or all planned Stratify concerns.
